When to see a doctor

By the CalJab team, with sources · Last reviewed October 20, 2026

The common digestive side effects mostly come when you start and when the dose goes up, and they ease over time. 1·2 Some signs, though, need help without waiting. 1·2·3

Get emergency help now

  • A serious allergic reaction: trouble breathing, fast swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, a very fast heartbeat, dizziness or fainting. 1·2·3
  • Vomit with blood, vomit that looks like coffee grounds, or green vomit. 6
  • Dehydration together with blue, grey or pale lips or skin, trouble breathing, confusion or being hard to wake. 7

Contact a doctor right away

  • Severe stomach pain that does not go away. It may spread to your back, with or without nausea or vomiting. It can be a sign of an inflamed pancreas. 1·2·3
  • Sudden loss of vision or eyesight that gets worse quickly. The Wegovy leaflet lists this as a very rare optic nerve problem (NAION). 1·4
  • Severe constipation with stomach ache, bloating and vomiting. The leaflet lists this as bowel obstruction among serious side effects. 1
  • Nausea, vomiting or diarrhea that does not go away, not keeping fluids down, dizziness on standing that does not pass, or peeing much less than usual. 3·6·7

Tell your doctor

  • Signs of gallbladder problems: pain in your upper stomach, fever, yellowing of the skin or eyes, clay-colored stools. 3
  • Your heart racing or pounding for several minutes. 3
  • A lump or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, trouble swallowing or shortness of breath. The US Medication Guide lists these as possible signs of a thyroid tumor. 3
  • If you have diabetes: changes in vision, or signs of low blood sugar such as cold sweats, shakiness, a fast heartbeat, hunger and dizziness. Low blood sugar is more likely with a sulfonylurea or insulin. 1·3
  • Any side effect that bothers you or does not go away. 4·5

Tell them ahead

  • Before surgery or a procedure under anaesthesia, say that you use this medicine. 1·2·3
  • If you are planning a pregnancy, talk to your doctor: semaglutide should be stopped at least 2 months before, tirzepatide at least 4 weeks before. 3·4·5
  • Don't stop your medicine without talking to your doctor. 1·4
