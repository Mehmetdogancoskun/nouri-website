Storing your pen and travelling

By the CalJab team, with sources · Last reviewed October 21, 2026

Storage rules depend on the brand and type of pen, so check the leaflet that comes with yours. 3 Pens you haven't started go in the fridge at 2 to 8 °C and must not be frozen. 1·2 Keep Wegovy away from the fridge's cooling element. 1

Wegovy

  • Single-dose pen: it can be kept out of the fridge for up to 28 days, at no more than 30 °C. 1 Keep it in its carton to protect it from light. 1
  • FlexTouch pen: after first use you can keep it for 6 weeks, below 30 °C or in the fridge. 1 Keep the cap on when you're not using it. 1
  • Don't use it if it has been frozen or the solution isn't clear and colourless. 1 Throw a single-dose pen away if it has been above 30 °C or out of the fridge for more than 28 days. 1
  • Wegovy tablets don't need any special temperature; keep them in the original package to protect them from light and moisture. 1

Mounjaro

  • Single-dose pen or vial: it can be kept out of the fridge below 30 °C for up to 21 days in total; after that it must be thrown away. 2 Keep it in the original packaging to protect it from light. 2
  • KwikPen: after first use, keep it in the fridge or below 30 °C, and throw it away 30 days after first use. 2
  • Don't use it if it has been frozen or is damaged, or if the medicine is cloudy, discoloured or has particles in it; it should be colourless to slightly yellow. 2

Needles

With FlexTouch and KwikPen, use a new needle for each injection and don't store the pen with a needle attached. 1·2

When you travel

  • Carry your pens in your hand luggage, in the original packaging, with a copy of your prescription and a letter from your prescriber. 5
  • Check storage needs before you go; you may need a way to keep them cool on the way. 5
  • If you're flying, contact the airline in advance about carrying injectable medicines. 5
  • Rules for bringing medicines into a country vary, and some countries need specific permission. 5
  • If travel makes your usual day hard, you can change your injection day as long as at least 3 days have passed since your last injection. 3·4
  • If you miss a dose, take Wegovy if it's 5 days or less since it was due, or Mounjaro if it's 4 days or less. 3·4 Otherwise skip it, and never take 2 doses together to make up for it. 3·4

In CalJab

The dose reminder follows the dose day set on your medicine in Health, so if you change your injection day, change it there too. Log each injection in Log dose; if you miss one, the missed dose card tells you what to do based on your medicine's leaflet.
