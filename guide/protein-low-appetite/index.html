Protein when your appetite is low

By the CalJab team, with sources · Last reviewed October 21, 2026

When you lose weight, you can lose muscle as well as fat. 1 In one study, about 38% of the weight lost was lean mass, including muscle. 1 Eating little protein because of low appetite can also add to muscle loss. 1

How much

  • The general adult recommendation is 0.8 g per kilogram a day; higher targets such as 1.2 to 1.6 g have been proposed during weight loss. 1
  • With obesity, it is not clear whether to use actual or adjusted body weight; using actual weight can overstate how much you need. 1
  • Long-term intake of 2 g per kilogram or more should be avoided. 1

Protein when you eat less

  • Choose lower-volume, nutrient-dense foods: fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, nuts and seeds, peanut or almond butter. 1
  • Some people top up with high-protein shakes, bars or fortified products. 1
  • Don't go too long without eating. Small, regular meals are advised. 1

Protein alone is not enough

To protect muscle you need regular strength training alongside protein; protein alone is likely not enough. 1 Aerobic exercise alone does less to protect muscle during fast weight loss. 1

If you use FODMAP mode

Most legumes are high FODMAP; eggs, firm tofu, tempeh and plain cooked meat, poultry and seafood are low FODMAP. 2 Marinated or processed meats may contain high FODMAP ingredients such as garlic and onion. 2
