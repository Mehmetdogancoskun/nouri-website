Consent

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Three separate consents. Each is given on its own screen with its own statement, and each can be withdrawn on its own. How your data is processed is explained separately in our Privacy Policy.

1. Health and nutrition records

Statement: "I consent to the processing of my health and nutrition records." Given with the checkbox on the account step, or once in a pop-up for existing users.

Scope: My meal records and meal photos, water, height, weight and body measurements, medication and dose records, symptoms and dietary preferences (such as halal and sensitive stomach) are processed by Mehmet Doğan Coşkun, the developer of CalJab, to:

  • keep my records and sync them across my devices,
  • calculate my calorie, protein, fiber and water goals,
  • show me summaries and reminders, and prepare a doctor report when I ask.

My records are stored on servers in Germany, visible only to me; they are not sold or used for advertising.

If I do not consent: CalJab cannot keep records, so no account is created. If I am an existing user, I can still view, download and delete my records, but I cannot add new ones.

Withdrawing: I can withdraw at any time by turning off the switch in Me > Privacy > Permissions and data. If I withdraw, my health and nutrition records are deleted; the app first suggests that I download them. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.

2. AI logging

Statement: By tapping "Allow" in the "Let OpenAI recognize the food?" window shown at my first AI request, I give explicit consent within the scope below.

Scope: When I use AI logging features, the content I send (a food or menu photo, a recipe link or text, a food sentence I type or say, a note I add) is transferred to OpenAI OpCo, LLC in the USA to recognize the foods and estimate their nutrition values.

  • My name, email, medication and dose details, weight, modes and Apple Health data are not sent.
  • OpenAI does not use this content to train its models; it keeps it for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring and then deletes it.
  • Content is sent only when I use an AI feature.

If I do not consent: Logging by hand, by barcode and from lists works the same.

Withdrawing: I can turn it off at any time in Me > Privacy > Permissions and data. After that, no content is sent.

3. Ask

Statement: By tapping "Allow" in the "Should Ask answer with your records?" window shown the first time I open Ask, I give explicit consent within the scope below.

Scope: When I ask Ask a question, my question, the last five questions and answers of that chat, the app language, the date and time on my phone and the parts of my records that are on in "What does Ask know?" (my medicine, dose and stage; my modes and foods I skip; my goals; today's meals; symptoms from the last 7 days; symptom patterns; my weight trend if I turn it on) are transferred to OpenAI OpCo, LLC in the USA, so that Ask can answer from CalJab's Guide in a way that fits me.

  • My name, email, age, sex, photos, Apple Health data and the questions and notes in my doctor report are not sent.
  • I can see what was sent in the "Knows" line under each question and turn off any part.
  • OpenAI does not use this content to train its models; it keeps it for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring and then deletes it.
  • My chats stay only on my phone. For each question the server keeps a usage record without text for 12 months. If I report a wrong answer, that chat is sent to the CalJab team and kept for 12 months.
  • Ask works only while my consent for health and nutrition records is on.

If I do not consent: I can read the Guide, and the rest of the app works the same.

Withdrawing: I can turn it off at any time in Me > Privacy > Permissions and data. After that nothing is sent, and my Ask usage records and reports are deleted.
