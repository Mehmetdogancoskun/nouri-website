How it works

Log a meal in a few seconds

No long forms. Use whatever is easiest right now.

1

Snap, say or scan

Take a photo, say what you ate in English, Turkish or Arabic, or scan a barcode. Describe home dishes the way you would to a friend.

2

Check and fix

Results are estimates, so you see every item before saving. Change a portion, rename a dish, or swap in a food from USDA FoodData Central.

3

See your day

Today shows protein first, then fiber and water. If you take a GLP-1 medicine, your dose day and how you feel sit next to your meals.

Features

What's inside

Made for people whose day is about more than calories.

📷

Photo logging

Snap your plate and CalJab lists each item with an estimate. You can undo a log for 5 seconds after saving.

🎤

Say it or type it

“I had two eggs and a slice of bread.” One sentence in English, Turkish or Arabic is enough.

📊

Targets that fit

Protein comes first. If you take a GLP-1 medicine, energy is a lower limit so you don't eat too little. Targets update when your weight changes, or you can set your own.

💊

Doses and symptoms

Log doses on an injection site map, get a reminder on dose day, and see what to do if you miss one, based on official product information. A short check-in builds your symptom map.

🔍

Barcode scanner

Looks up packaged foods in Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central.

🍽️

Plans and a shopping list

Plan the week with recipes that follow your modes. The shopping list fills from your plan, and you can share it.

⚖️

Weight

Log your weight and see how it changes week by week. Weigh-ins can be saved to Apple Health.

📱

Home Screen widgets

Your day, your next meal, water and your dose day, without opening the app.

Who it's for

Three modes. Pick any, or none.

Modes are optional and you can change them later. Without them, CalJab is a simple food tracker.

💉

GLP-1 medicines

A dose log with an injection site map and a next-site suggestion, dose-day reminders, missed-dose guidance from official product information, and an estimated medicine level. Protein comes first, and energy has a lower limit.

Ozempic · Wegovy · Mounjaro · and more
🌙

Halal

Meal plans leave out recipes that aren't halal, and menu picks leave out dishes that aren't. Photo results can show a halal note, which is an estimate. CalJab does not certify products; the Guide explains how to check a certificate.

Low FODMAP
