Nutrition Guide

Knowledge built into every feature

We don't just track — we help you understand. Here's what we've learned building for these communities.

🥩 Protein on GLP-1: Why it's critical

GLP-1 medications suppress appetite, often causing inadequate protein intake and muscle loss. Aim for 1.2–1.8g per kg daily. At 70kg, that's 84–126g. Most users undershoot by 30–40% without tracking.

💧 Hydration during GLP-1 treatment

Nausea and reduced appetite mean less water intake. Dehydration risks are real — especially the first 48 hours after injection. Aim for 2.5–3L daily. CalJab's GLP-1 mode increases your water target automatically.

🌿 FODMAP basics: Finding your triggers

FODMAPs are fermentable carbs that cause bloating, gas, and pain. Common triggers: onion, garlic, wheat, certain fruits. Tracking meals alongside symptoms reveals your personal pattern within 2–3 weeks.

☪️ Halal nutrition: Beyond the label

Halal isn't just about meat. Additives like gelatin, mono/diglycerides, and certain colorings can be non-halal. CalJab checks ingredient lists automatically — so you eat with full confidence.
