Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What CalJab collects and why, who it is shared with, and your rights.

In short

CalJab is developed by Mehmet Doğan Coşkun, who is responsible for your data ("we", the data controller).

  • Your data is never sold and never used for advertising. The app has no ads and no third-party tracking tools.
  • Your health and nutrition records are processed only with your explicit consent and are stored on servers in Germany, visible only to you.
  • AI logging works only with a separate permission and sends OpenAI only the photo or sentence you send. Ask, the assistant in Premium, has its own permission and sends your question with the parts of your records you leave on.
  • You can download your data from the app at any time, or delete it together with your account.
  • Questions: support@caljab.app.

How we use it and our legal basis

  • Providing the service (account, sync, plans, lists, subscription): our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Processing your health and nutrition records (logging, goals, summaries, reminders, doctor report): your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a); KVKK Art. 6(2)). Your halal preference may reveal a belief, so it is covered by the same consent.
  • AI logging: your separate explicit consent.
  • Ask: your separate explicit consent. A chat you report is sent under the same consent when you confirm sending it.
  • Fixing bugs, improving the app and preventing abuse (crash reports, usage events, security logs): our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). This data contains no health information.
  • Legal obligations: where required.

Your goals are suggested by a calculation. We make no automated decision that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you, and you can change every goal.

AI: what is sent and what is not

When you log with a photo, a menu, a recipe or by typing, the content goes through our server to OpenAI (USA). Foods and estimated nutrition values come back, and you confirm the entry.

  • Sent: the photo or sentence, any note you add, the app language.
  • Not sent: your name, email, account ID, medication and dose details, weight, modes, Apple Health data.
  • OpenAI does not use this content to train its models and keeps it for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • When you log by voice, Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text; only the text, never the audio, goes to OpenAI.
  • These features ask for a separate permission the first time you use them. You can turn it off in Me > Privacy; logging by hand, by barcode and from lists keeps working.
  • AI results are estimates and can be wrong.

Ask: what is sent and what is not

Ask is the assistant in the Assistant tab. It is part of Premium; free accounts can try it with a limited number of questions. It answers short questions about eating, your medicine routine, symptoms and how to use CalJab, using CalJab's sourced Guide articles. Your question goes through our server to OpenAI (USA), and the answer comes back to your phone.

  • Sent with each question: the question, the last five questions and answers of that chat, the app language, the date and time on your phone, and the parts of your records that are on in "What does Ask know?": your medicine, dose and stage (for example Ozempic 0.5 mg, dose increase), your modes and foods you skip, your goals, today's meals (name and protein), on how many of the last 7 days you logged each symptom, symptom patterns (Premium) and your weight trend (off unless you turn it on).
  • Never sent: your name, email, account ID, age, sex, photos, Apple Health data, and the questions and notes in your doctor report.
  • The "Knows" line under each question shows what was sent. You can turn off any part in "What does Ask know?"; this does not change your records in the app.
  • OpenAI does not use this content to train its models and keeps it for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • Your chats are kept only on your phone. They are not in iCloud backups, do not move to a new phone and are deleted when you sign out. Our server does not keep the text of your questions or of the answers.
  • For each question our server keeps a usage record without any text: the date, the kind of answer shown, how much text was processed and the cost. It is used for question limits, costs and preventing abuse, kept for 12 months and included in your data download.
  • If you tap "Report wrong information" and confirm, the questions and answers of that chat are sent to us so that we can fix the mistake. Nothing else from your records is sent. We keep a report for 12 months.
  • When you ask by voice, Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text, on your phone where the phone supports it; only the text is sent.
  • Ask asks for its own permission the first time you open it. You can turn it off in Me > Privacy > Permissions and data; after that nothing is sent, and the usage records and reports above are deleted. They are also deleted if you withdraw your health consent.
  • Ask gives general information, not medical advice. It does not suggest doses or dose changes, and when it recognizes signs of an emergency it shows your local emergency number instead of an answer. Answers can be wrong.

Retention, deletion and your rights

  • Your data is kept while your account is open. If you delete an entry, its photo is deleted with it.
  • You can delete your account in Me > Privacy > Permissions and data. Your data is deleted immediately and drops out of daily backups within 7 days. If you withdraw your health consent, your health and nutrition records are deleted in the same way.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days, usage events for 12 months and server security logs for at most 7 days. Content sent with AI stays with OpenAI for at most 30 days. Ask usage records and reported chats are kept for 12 months; your Ask chats stay only on your phone.
  • Your rights: to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability (by downloading it in the app), and to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before.
  • To use them, write to support@caljab.app; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
  • Users in Turkey also have the KVKK information notice (in Turkish).

Children, security, changes and contact

  • Children: CalJab is not for anyone under 18; no account is created if the birth year at setup shows an age under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete the account and its data.
  • Security: Connections are encrypted (TLS), data is stored on encrypted disks, and each user can reach only their own data. Meal photos sit in a folder only you can access. Medication names are hidden on the lock screen by default.
  • Changes: If we change this policy we update its date. We announce significant changes in the app, and ask for your consent again if its scope changes.
